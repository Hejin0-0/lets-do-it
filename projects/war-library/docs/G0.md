`docs/DESIGN.md` is the design. **`src/contract/*.ts` is the contract and it wins** wherever the
two disagree: DESIGN §11.3 was compressed after the contract was frozen and lists a subset of
its fields. Build to the files, not the prose.

## Coordinates and scale (real metres)

- Table centre at the world origin; tabletop surface at `TABLE_TOP_Y = 0.9`. `RoomView.tableTop`
  is an `Object3D` at `(0, 0.9, 0)`; `BoardView.mount(tableTop)` parents the board to it.
- Board-local frame: +x east, +z south, origin = board centre, y = 0 at the painted surface.
- Hexes: flat-top, odd-q offset, `HEX_FLAT = 0.1` m across the flats, circumradius
  `R = 0.1/√3`. Column spacing `1.5R`, row spacing `0.1`, odd columns (B, D, F…) shifted +0.05
  south. `HexId = row*13 + col`, row 0 = north (German side), col 0 = west (the sea). The board
  is 1.155 × 0.95 m. The greybox `local()` in `src/board/BoardView.ts` is the reference formula.
- Commander camera (Lead, `src/systems/CameraRig.ts`): pitch 45°, vertical FOV 36°, **1.95 m**
  from a target at (0, 0.9, +0.06), yaw ±35°. Zoom stops: close 1.15 m / 52°, gallery 4.1 m / 34°.
  (Changed from 1.55 m after integration: 1.55 put the board at 94% of the width. At 1.95 m the
  mounted board fills ~70% — measure the ≥28 px piece gate at THIS distance.)

## Verifying your own work (you cannot see it unless you render it)

- Type check: `./node_modules/.bin/tsc --noEmit -p .` — while four people build at once, the
  tree may carry someone else's errors; **you are done when none of the errors are in your files.**
- Rules: `npm run test:rules` (Node 22 runs `.ts` directly; relative imports inside `src/rules`,
  `src/ai`, `src/contract` MUST use the `.ts` extension and `import type` for types).
- Rendering: start your own dev server in the background on YOUR port and capture with the
  bundled CDP probe (headless Chrome, no extra dependencies):
  - B: `npx vite --port 5191 --strictPort`  ·  C: `--port 5192`  ·  D: `--port 5193`
  - `node scripts/probe.mjs --url "http://127.0.0.1:5192/?room&view=overview" --w 1280 --h 720 --wait 8000 --shot /tmp/c-room.png --eval "JSON.stringify(window.__THREE_INFO__||{})"`
  - then open the PNG with the Read tool and LOOK at it. Crop/zoom with
    `sips -c H W --cropOffset TOP LEFT in.png --out crop.png`. Flaws hide at full-frame size.
  - Stop your dev server when you finish.
- Never modify anything outside `war-library/`. Never run git. No new npm dependencies.

## House rules (from the old build's defects — docs/research/digest_reuse-audit.md §d)

- Every capacity limit (instance counts, pools) THROWS on overflow; nothing is dropped silently.
- Anything shown by colour also has a shape or icon.
- Measure, don't assume: a screenshot you have not opened is not evidence.
- No `Math.random`, `Date.now()` or `performance.now()` in rules/AI. Cosmetic randomness in views
  uses a seeded generator (`src/utils/random.ts`, `createSeededRandom`) so captures reproduce.
- three r184: `RoomEnvironment()` takes no arguments; `scene.environmentIntensity` exists; the
  post chain is `EffectComposer → RenderPass → UnrealBloomPass → ShaderPass(vignette/grain) →
  OutputPass` from `three/addons/...`. Never use `transmission`.
